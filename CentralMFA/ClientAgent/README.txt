========================================================================
   CentralMFA - Mantra MFS100 Client Workstation Scanner Agent Setup
========================================================================

QUICK 2-STEP INSTALLATION:

1. Extract all files from this ZIP file to a folder on your computer.
2. Right-click on 'install.bat' and choose 'Run as administrator'.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT:
- The service will install to C:\Program Files\CentralMFA_Agent\
- The scanner agent (MfaScannerAgent.exe) will start immediately on port 8034.
- It will automatically start whenever your computer powers on.
- Your browser on CentralMFA will automatically detect your Mantra MFS100 scanner!

TROUBLESHOOTING:
- Ensure your Mantra MFS100 USB cable is firmly plugged into a working USB port.
- If Windows Firewall prompts you, click "Allow Access" for Private Networks.
